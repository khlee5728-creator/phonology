Quick Review — From Week 11

Maximal Onset Principle (§6.4)

Assign as many consonants as possible to the onset of the following syllable, as long as the result is a permissible onset.

/ˈsiː.krɪt/ ← secret (not /ˈsiːk.rɪt/)

/ˈɛks.trə/ ← extra

/ˈwɪn.tɚ/ ← winter (/nt/ invalid onset → /n/ stays coda)

Sonority Hierarchy (§6.3)

Sounds rank by relative openness; nucleus = sonority peak. Number of peaks = number of syllables.

Stops < Fricatives < Nasals < Liquids < Glides < Vowels

10-point scale: low V=10, mid V=9, high V/glide=8, flap=7, lateral=6, nasal=5, voiced fric=4, voiceless fric=3, voiced stop=2, voiceless stop=1.

→ This week: how does sonority constrain English clusters? (SSP, §6.5.2)

§6.5 English Syllable Phonotactics — Introduction

Language-specific syllabification

E.g., acne /ækni/ — English splits as /æk.ni/ (since /kn/ is not a permissible onset). Other languages allow /kn/: Norwegian Knut, Hebrew Knesset, Russian kniga, German Knabe.

General formula for English syllables

(C) (C) (C) V (C) (C) (C) {C}

V (vowel/diphthong) is the only obligatory element. The 4th coda C is bracketed because it must come from a suffix (e.g., bursts /bɝsts/).

Structure examples (Yavaş p. 161–162)
CVsay [se]VCat [æt]
CCVpray [pɹe]VCCact [ækt]
CCCVspray [spɹe]VCCCnext [nɛkst]
CVCbeat [bit]CVCCtext [tɛkst]
CCVCbreak [bɹek]CCVCCprint [pɹɪnt]
CCCVCstrike [stɹaɪk]CVCCCsphinx [sfɪŋks]

With suffix: sprints (CCCVCCC), worlds (CVCCCC), twelfths (CCVCCCC).

§6.5 도입 (교재 pp.161–162). 핵심 메시지 두 가지: (1) syllabification은 언어별 — 'acne' /ækni/는 영어에서 /æk.ni/로 분음절(/kn/ onset 불가), 그러나 노르웨이어 Knut, 히브리어 Knesset, 러시아어 kniga, 독일어 Knabe는 /kn/ onset 허용. (2) 영어 syllable의 일반 공식 (C)(C)(C)V(C)(C)(C){C} — V만 obligatory, 4번째 coda는 접미사일 때만 가능 (예: bursts /bɝsts/). 다양한 구조: CV(say), CCV(pray), CCCV(spray), VC(at), VCC(act), VCCC(next), CVC(beat), CCVC(break), CCCVC(strike), CVCC(text), CCVCCC(print), CVCCCC(sphinx); 접미사 포함: sprints(CCCVCCC), worlds(CVCCCC), twelfths(CCVCCCC). 다음 슬라이드부터 onset/coda 각각의 세부 제약 다룸.

§6.5.1 Single onsets — Restricted Consonants

Most English consonants can serve as a single onset. Three exceptions:

/ŋ/ — never in onset

Restricted to coda position only (sing, ring, finger). No English word begins with /ŋ-/.

/ʒ/ — restricted onset

Doesn't begin native English words. Occurs in foreign loans (genre, Zhivago) and word-internally (vision, measure).

/ð/ — function words only

Word-initial /ð-/ only in grammatical function words: the, then, there, this, that. Content words begin with /θ/ (think, thank).

교재 §6.5.1. 영어 단일 자음 onset의 세 가지 제약: (1) /ŋ/는 onset 위치 절대 불가 — 항상 coda. 한국어 'ㅇ' (받침) 위치와 유사. 일부 언어에서는 /ŋ/가 onset 가능 (Vietnamese 'Nguyen'). (2) /ʒ/는 영어 고유 단어에서는 거의 onset에 안 옴. 외래어(genre, jeune) 또는 단어 내부에서 (vision, measure, garage). (3) /ð/ word-initial은 기능어(function words)에 한정 — the, this, that, then, there, them. 내용어(content words)는 무성 /θ/로 시작 (think, thank, three). 학생들에게 /ŋ-/로 시작하는 영어 단어를 떠올려보라고 하면 없음을 깨닫게 됨.

§6.5.2 Double onsets — Restrictions & Summary

Affricates are the only class of consonants that do not appear in onset clusters. Beyond that, 11 systematic restrictions apply (Yavaş pp. 162–163):

교재 §6.5.2 (pp.162–163) 11개 systematic restrictions를 3개 범주(C₂, C₁, C₁+C₂)로 정리. (1) Affricates는 onset cluster에 절대 안 옴. (2) C₂ 제약 4개: 유성 마찰음 불가, 유성 폐쇄음 불가, /f/ 외 마찰음 불가, 폐쇄음·비음은 /s/ 뒤에만. (3) C₁ 제약 3개: non-lateral approximant 불가 + /l/은 /j/ 앞만 (lure), /h, m/은 /j/ 앞만 (huge, music), /θ/는 /ɹ, w/ 앞만 (three, thwart). (4) C₁+C₂ 공기 제약 4개: /s/-/ʃ/ complementary (/ʃɹ/ shrimp), labial-labial 불가, geminate 불가, alveolar stop + /l/ 불가 (✗ /tl-, dl-/). (5) 두 패턴 요약: (a) /s/+C — SSP 위반, adjunct; (b) obstruent + approximant — SSP 따름 (다음 슬라이드).

§6.5.2 Double onsets — Sonority Sequencing Principle (SSP)

SSP — Selkirk (1984: 116)

"...a segment constituting a sonority peak ... preceded and/or followed by a sequence of segments with progressively decreasing sonority values."

Valid onsets (rising sonority)

/pl-/stop [p] (1) < liquid [l] (6) ✓
/fr-/fricative [f] (3) < liquid [r] (7) ✓
/sm-/fricative [s] (3) < nasal [m] (5) ✓
/kw-/stop [k] (1) < glide [w] (8) ✓

Invalid onsets (violate SSP)

*/lp-/liquid [l] (6) > stop [p] (1) ✗
*/ml-/nasal [m] (5) > liquid [l] (6) — too small jump ✗
*/nt-/nasal [n] (5) > stop [t] (1) ✗
Exception: /s/ in English! /st-, sp-, sk-, str-, spl-/ all violate SSP — /s/ (3) is less sonorous than a stop (1) but can precede one. These are treated as "extrasyllabic" or "appendix" consonants.
교재 §6.5.2. SSP는 음절 구조의 보편 원리로, Selkirk (1984)에 의해 공식화됨. 핵심: 음절 내 sonority는 nucleus를 향해 상승하고 멀어지면 하강. 영어의 /s-stop/ 결합(/sp/, /st/, /sk/)은 SSP 위반이지만 허용됨 — /s/가 음절 외부(extrasyllabic) 또는 부록(appendix) 위치에 있다고 분석. 이것은 언어 보편론과 언어 특수성의 흥미로운 긴장을 보여줌. 학생들에게 'play'와 *'lpay'를 발음시키며 sonority 흐름 비교 권장.

§6.5.4 Codas — Single Codas (Restrictions)

Most consonants can occur in coda — with a few exceptions

Coda phonotactics are more permissive than onset phonotactics.

/h/ — never in coda

Mirror image of /ŋ/: /h/ is onset-only, /ŋ/ is coda-only.

/ʒ/ — less solid in coda

garage [gəˈɹɑdʒ], massage [məˈsɑdʒ] — some speakers realize as [dʒ].

/j/, /w/ — debated

Often said to be excluded from coda, but diphthong endings /aɪ ɔɪ aʊ/ are essentially equivalent to /j w/ (some transcribe /aj aw ɔj/).

교재 §6.5.4 (p. 165). 영어 단일 coda 제약: (1) /h/는 절대 coda 불가 (onset-only) — /ŋ/와 거울상. (2) /ʒ/는 다소 약한 위치 — garage, massage에서 [dʒ]로도 실현됨. (3) /j/, /w/는 종종 coda 불가로 언급되지만 이중모음 /aɪ ɔɪ aʊ/의 끝부분이 /j w/와 사실상 동일 — 일부 표기 체계는 /aj aw ɔj/로 표기.

Voicing agreement 원칙 — /spt/ lisped처럼 장애음 cluster는 voicing 일치.

§6.5 English Syllable Phonotactics — Cluster Simplification (1/2): Word-boundary Deletion

Word-final /nd/, /st/, /ft/ delete before another consonant

In rapid connected speech the final stop drops when the next word begins with a consonant. Same pattern applies in suffixes/compounds.

Deletion across word boundary
  • hand made [hæn med]
  • spend money [spɛn mʌni]
  • next class [nɛks klæs]
  • just now [dʒʌs naʊ]
  • left street [lɛf stɹ̣it]

Within words too: handsome [hænsəm], textless [tɛksləs], softness [sɔfnəs].

Three constraints (Yavaş p. 168)
  • Blocked by /h/: wild horse [waɪld hɔɹ̣s], guest house [gɛst haʊs]
  • Optional before liquid: hand luggage [hæn(d) lʌgədʒ], guest list
  • Grammatical endings preserved: planned trip, fixed game, autographed book
교재 §6.5 (pp.167–168) cluster simplification 1부. Word-boundary deletion: 어말 /nd/(hand made, spend money, grand bargain), /st/(next class, just now, best thing), /ft/(left street) 마지막 자음이 다음 단어 첫 자음 앞에서 탈락. 동일 패턴이 suffix/compound 내에서도 (handsome, textless, softness). 3가지 제약: (1) /h/ 차단(wild horse, guest house), (2) 유음 앞 선택적(hand luggage), (3) grammatical ending 보존(planned trip, fixed game). 빠른 발화일수록 단순화 빈도 증가. 다음 슬라이드: fast-speech reduced vowel deletion + AAVE 방언 변이.

Reduced-vowel deletion creates "impermissible" clusters; AAVE adds dialectal variation

Fast-speech reduction of unstressed vowels yields onset clusters disallowed in citation form. AAVE shows additional simplification + reordering patterns.

§6.5 English Syllable Phonotactics — Accidental vs. Systematic Gaps (supplementary)

Accidental Gap

Phonotactically permissible — just no word happens to have that form.

/blɪk/ — sounds English-like ✓

/prɪm/ — sounds English-like ✓

/flæʃ/ — actually flash exists!

Speakers recognize these as possible English words.

Systematic Gap

Phonotactically excluded — violates English phonotactic rules.

*/bnɪk/ — /bn-/ onset ✗

*/tlæʃ/ — /tl-/ onset ✗

*/ŋɪp/ — /ŋ-/ onset ✗

Speakers feel these are impossible in English.

이 구분은 심리언어학적으로 중요합니다. 영어 화자는 우연적 공백 단어는 '이상하지만 영어 단어처럼 들린다'고 느끼고, 체계적 공백 단어는 '영어 단어처럼 들리지 않는다'고 느낍니다. Vitevitch & Luce(1999) 등의 실험 연구에서 이러한 직관을 측정했습니다. 교수 팁: 학생들에게 무작위 음 연쇄를 들려주고 '영어처럼 들리나?'를 판단하게 하는 활동이 효과적입니다.

§6.6 Written Syllabification — Two Principles

Written breaks ≠ spoken syllabification

Dictionary hyphenation follows two written principles, often differing from phonological (MOP-based) syllabification.

Two principles of written syllabification

(a) Prefix/suffix integrity: morphemes cannot be divided
boat·ers, sav·ing, un·able

(b) Vowel-length rule: when a, e, i, o, u, y stand for...

  • long vowel/diphthong → next consonant goes to following syllable: pe·nal, mi·ner
  • short vowel → next consonant stays with preceding syllable: pen·al·ty, min·er·al

Written breaks vs. spoken syllabifications

→ Written hyphenation differs from MOP-based phonological breaks.

Principle (a) is stronger: morpheme integrity overrides the vowel-length rule → boat·ers, sav·ing, un·able.

Morphological pairs (vowel-length rule): pe·nal / pen·al·ty · mi·ner / min·er·al · pu·ni·tive / pun·ish · gra·di·ent / grad·u·al

교재 §6.6 (pp.169–171). 사전 하이픈 위치는 음성학적 음절 경계와 다름. 두 가지 written principle: (a) 접두사·접미사 분리 불가 (boat-ers, sav-ing, un-able 같은 단어의 형태소 경계 유지); (b) 모음 길이 기반 — orthographic letter가 long V/diphthong 표시하면 다음 자음은 다음 음절로 (pe-nal), short V 표시하면 다음 자음은 같은 음절에 남음 (pen-al-ty). 이 원칙이 형태론적 페어에서 명확히 드러남: penal/penalty, miner/mineral, punitive/punish, gradient/gradual. 핵심 메시지: written syllabification은 발화 음절과 무관 — 영어 강세 규칙은 spoken syllable에 기반. 학생들에게 사전 하이픈을 신뢰하지 말라고 강조.

§6.7 Syllable Weight and Ambisyllabicity — Light vs. Heavy Syllables

Weight is determined by rhyme structure (Yavaş p. 172)

Light: non-branching rhyme — short V, no coda (e.g., first σ of around). Heavy: branching rhyme — short V + coda or long V/diphthong (± coda).

Light vs. Heavy rhymes (Yavaş p. 172)

(a) light      (b) heavy        (c) heavy      (d) heavy
   σ              σ                σ              σ
  / \            / \              / \            / \
 O   R          O   R            O   R          O   R
     |          |  / \           |   |          |  / \
     N          |  N   C         |   N          |  N   C
     |          |  |  / \        |  / \         | / \  |
     ə          n  ɛ  s  t       s  i  ː        s a  ɪ d
"a·mong"      "nest"            "sea"          "side"
Three heavy patterns
  • Short V + coda (CVC, CVCC): net, nest
  • Long V/diphthong, no coda (CVV): sea
  • Long V/diphthong + coda (CVVC): side, seed

Heavy syllables attract stress. In English, "no stressed syllable is light" — leading to the ambisyllabicity solution (next slide).

교재 §6.7 (p. 172). 음절 무게는 rhyme 구조로 결정. Light = non-branching rhyme (short V + no coda) — 예: around의 첫 음절 [ə]. Heavy = branching rhyme 세 가지 패턴: (1) short V + coda — net, nest; (2) long V/diphthong + no coda — sea; (3) long V/diphthong + coda — side, seed. 트리 4개: (a) among = light, (b) nest = heavy (branching coda), (c) sea = heavy (branching nucleus), (d) side = heavy (branching nucleus + coda). 핵심 원리: heavy 음절이 강세를 끌어들임. 영어에서 "강세를 받은 음절은 light일 수 없다" — 이 원리가 다음 슬라이드의 ambisyllabicity 동기.

§6.7 Syllable Weight and Ambisyllabicity — Ambisyllabicity (Light-Stress Conflict)

When stress falls on a light syllable → conflict

MOP makes σ₁ of medicine, happen, finish light, but stress falls on it. No stressed syllable in English is light. Solution: the C becomes ambisyllabic — both coda of σ₁ and onset of σ₂.

Ambisyllabic words (textbook §6.7)

medicinefederal originpositive happenCanada finishriver (cf. rival) funnypunish (cf. punitive)

All have stressed lax vowel (/ɛ, ɔ, æ, ʌ, ɪ/) + single intervocalic C.

Rule: A C is ambisyllabic when it follows a stressed lax vowel /ɪ ɛ æ ʌ ʊ ɔ/ɑ/. → Stressed rhyme becomes heavy (branching).

교재 §6.7 (pp.171–173). 핵심 동기: MOP 적용 시 medicine, happen 등의 첫 음절이 light가 되지만 강세가 그 음절에 붙어 conflict 발생 ("영어에서 강세를 받은 음절은 light일 수 없다"). 해결책: 자음이 ambisyllabic — 동시에 첫 음절의 coda이자 둘째 음절의 onset. 조건: 짧은(lax) 모음 (/ɪ ɛ æ ʌ ʊ ɔ ɑ/)이 stressed syllable의 nucleus일 때, 그 다음에 오는 자음. 단어 리스트: medicine, origin, happen, finish, funny, federal, positive, Canada, river, punish — 모두 강세 lax + 단일 intervocalic C 패턴. 미국 영어 flapping (bitter [bɪɾɚ])도 이 환경.

§6.8 Practical Applications — Sonority-driven Cluster Reduction (1/2): Concept

Cluster reduction prefers greater sonority jumps

When clusters are reduced, the result maximizes sonority distance from onset to nucleus. The largest C→V rise is most "CV-like" — and CV is universally unmarked.

Example: play [ple]

Target: [ple] (onset /pl/ + /e/)

  • If reduced to [pe]: /p/(1) → /e/(9) — jump of 8 ✓
  • If reduced to *[le]: /l/(6) → /e/(9) — jump of only 3 ✗

→ [pe] is preferred because the larger sonority jump is more "CV-like" (universally unmarked).

/sC/ cluster reduction difficulty
ClusterC₂ classSonority jumpEasier?
/sw-/glide (8)3 → 8 = 5✅ easiest
/sl-/liquid (6)3 → 6 = 3medium
/sn-/nasal (5)3 → 5 = 2medium
/st-/stop (1)3 → 1 = −2❌ hardest

Jump size predicts production accuracy (Yavaş et al. 2005–2010).

교재 §6.8 (pp.173–176) 핵심 개념. CV가 가장 unmarked한 음절 구조 → C-V 사이 sonority jump가 클수록 "CV-like"하므로 선호. 예: target play를 [le]로 줄이는 것보다 [pe]로 보존하는 것이 일반적. 이유: [pe]의 /p/(1)→/e/(9)=8 vs [le]의 /l/(6)→/e/(9)=3. /sC/ 군에서도 동일 패턴: /sw/(jump 5) > /sl/(3) > /sn/(2) > /st/(−2 = SSP 위반). 빈도와 별개로 sonority distance가 정확률을 예측한다는 것이 본 슬라이드의 핵심. 다음 슬라이드에서 cross-population 증거 (아동 음운장애, aphasia, 정상 습득) 제시.

§6.8 Practical Applications — Sonority-driven Cluster Reduction (2/2): Cross-population evidence

Same sonority-driven pattern across populations

L1 acquisition, clinical populations, and L2 learning all show: bigger sonority jump = easier production.

Child phonological disorders (Chin 1996)

Cluster reduction applied only when sonority distance C₁→C₂ < 3; distance ≥ 3 is preserved.

Surviving consonant produces the maximum jump to the nucleus.

Aphasia studies

Same sonority preference in aphasic cluster simplification:

  • Blumstein (1978)
  • Beland et al. (1990)
  • Christman (1992)
  • Romani & Calabrese (1998)
Normal acquisition — coda reduction (Ohala 1999)

Children (1;9 – 3;2) reducing CVCC keep the higher-sonority C, preserving minimum descent from nucleus to coda.

e.g. [maɹp] → [maɹ] (10→7) ✓ preferred over *[map] (10→1) ✗.

Frequency exception (Yavaş & Core; Yavaş & Beaubrun 2006)

/st/ shows the highest correct realization despite violating SSP; /sn/ the lowest.

Lexical frequency may override sonority distance for /sC/. Topic needs further investigation.

교재 §6.8 (pp.173–176) cross-population 증거. (1) Chin (1996): 음운장애 아동의 cluster reduction — 살아남는 C는 vowel까지의 sonority jump이 최대인 segment. (2) Aphasia: Blumstein 1978, Beland et al. 1990, Christman 1992, Romani & Calabrese 1998 — 동일한 sonority 선호 패턴. (3) Ohala (1999): 정상 아동 1;9–3;2, CCVC뿐 아니라 coda에서도 minimum descent 선호 — [maɹp]→[maɹ] (10→7) preferred, *[map] (10→1) avoided. (4) Yavaş & Core, Yavaş & Beaubrun (2006): /sC/ frequency exception — /st/는 SSP 위반에도 정확률 최고, /sn/은 최저. 빈도 효과가 sonority distance를 override할 수 있음 (교재 명시: topic needs further investigation). 핵심 결론: sonority sequencing은 보편적 — 정상 acquisition, 음운장애, aphasia 모두 같은 원리.

§6.8 Practical Applications — Sonority in Children's Developing Phonologies

Sonority-driven cluster reduction in normal and disordered acquisition

Children's cluster simplifications follow the maximum sonority jump principle — the same constraint at work in adult phonology and clinical populations.

Chin (1996) — children with phonological disorders

When a cluster reduces, the surviving C maximizes the sonority jump to the nucleus.

Patterns observed:

  • (a) stop+approx → stop: twin → [tɪn], drum → [dʌm], play → [pe]
  • (b) fricative+sonorant → fricative: few → [fu], swim → [sɪm], shrub → [ʃʌb]
  • (c) fricative+stop → stop: spoon → [pun], stove → [tov], sky → [kaɪ]

In each case, the C with the larger sonority distance to V is retained.

Ohala (1999) — normal children (1;9 – 3;2)

CCVC targets reduced to CVC: children deleted the more sonorous member, preserving max sonority rise to nucleus.

CVCC coda reduction: kept higher-sonority C, preserving minimum descent.

e.g. [maɹp] → [maɹ] (10→7) preferred over *[map] (10→1).

Bilingual evidence (Yavaş studies)

Yavaş & Someillan (2005, Sp-Eng), Yavaş & Barlow (2006), Yavaş & McLeod (2010): #sC cluster success ordered by sonority jump — /sw/ (3→8) ≫ /sl/ (3→6).

교재 §6.8 (pp.173–175). 아동 언어 발달의 cluster reduction은 sonority-driven 원리를 따름. (1) Chin (1996): 음운장애 아동 — cluster가 reduce될 때 살아남는 C는 vowel까지의 sonority jump이 최대인 segment. 구체 패턴 (a) stop+approx→stop (twin→tɪn, drum→dʌm, play→pe), (b) fric+sonorant→fric (few→fu, swim→sɪm, shrub→ʃʌb), (c) fric+stop→stop (spoon→pun, stove→tov, sky→kaɪ). 핵심: 더 큰 sonority distance를 가진 C가 보존. (2) Ohala (1999): 1;9–3;2 정상 아동, CCVC→CVC에서 더 sonorous한 C 삭제 (max rise to nucleus). CVCC→CVC에서는 [maɹp]→[maɹ] (10→7) 선호, *[map] (10→1) 회피. (3) Yavaş 시리즈 (2005, 2006, 2010): bilingual 아동 #sC cluster 정확률은 sonority jump 순 — /sw/ ≫ /sl/ ≫ /sn/, /sp/ (단 C2가 continuant일 때만 유의). 핵심 메시지: sonority sequencing은 보편적 — 정상·장애 acquisition 모두에서 동일한 원리 작동.

  • Onset clusters (Table 6.1): 11 systematic restrictions; affricates excluded; SSP requires rising sonority (/s/-clusters are "adjuncts")
