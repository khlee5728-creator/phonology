§7.1 Introduction — What is Stress?

Acoustic Correlates

  • Higher pitch (F₀ increase)
  • Greater intensity (louder)
  • Longer duration
  • Fuller vowel quality (less reduction)

Duration is the most reliable correlate in English.

Phonological Role

  • English is a stress-timed language (cf. mora-timed: Japanese)
  • Stress is lexically contrastive: changes word identity
  • Unstressed syllables undergo vowel reduction → /ə/

pɜ́ː.mɪt (noun) vs. pɜːmɪ́t (verb)

강세는 단순히 '크게 말하는 것'이 아닙니다. 음향적으로 음고(F₀), 강도(dB), 지속(duration), 모음 질(vowel quality)의 복합적 효과입니다. 영어에서는 특히 지속 시간이 강세의 가장 안정적인 단서입니다. 영어는 stress-timed 언어로, 강세 음절 사이의 시간 간격이 비교적 일정하려는 경향이 있습니다. 이는 한국어(syllable-timed)와 다르므로, 한국인 학습자에게 영어 리듬이 어색하게 느껴지는 이유 중 하나입니다.

교재 Yavaš (2020)는 강세 표기에 acute (´)와 grave (`) 다이어크리틱을 모음 위에 사용. 1차 강세는 acute, 2차 강세는 grave. [ə]는 weightless하여 강세를 받을 수 없음. 강의 자료는 교재 스타일을 따르므로 모든 IPA 표기에서 acute/grave 사용. IPA 사전 스타일(ˈ, ˌ before syllable)은 별도 참고 사항으로 알려주되 강의 자료에서는 사용하지 않음. 학생들에게 두 표기법 모두 인지하도록 안내 (사전이나 다른 자료에서 ˈ/ˌ을 볼 수 있음).

§7.1 Introduction — Variable & Mobile Stress

English has variable stress (any syllable in a polysyllabic word can carry stress) and mobile stress (morphologically related words shift stress + accompanying vowel reduction). (Yavaš p. 179)

Variable stress — trisyllabic nouns

Stress can fall on 1st, 2nd, or 3rd syllable:

article[áɹtɪkḷ]1st
tomato[təméɹo]2nd
kangaroo[kæ̀ŋgəɹ̣ú]3rd

교재 §7.1 (pp.179) Variable + Mobile stress 소개. (1) Variable: 영어 강세는 polysyllabic 단어의 어느 음절에든 올 수 있음 — article (1st), tomato (2nd), kangaroo (3rd). 게르만 언어 특성. (2) Mobile: 형태론적으로 관련된 단어들이 다른 음절로 강세가 이동 + 동시에 vowel reduction 동반. 핵심 시리즈: democrat/democracy/democratic, origin/original/originality, constitute/constitutional/constitutionality, photograph/photography/photographic, diplomat/diplomacy/diplomatic. 한국 학생들에게: 강세 위치가 단지 음향적 특성이 아니라 모음 질까지 바꿈을 강조. 다음 슬라이드부터 §7.2 명사·형용사 강세 규칙 (penult rule).

§7.1 Introduction — Stressable Syllable & Syllable Positions

Before formulating stress rules, two preliminaries are needed: what counts as a stressable syllable, and how to refer to syllable positions in a word. (Yavaš p. 180)

Stressable syllable (= heavy)
  • Light: non-branching rhyme (short V, no coda) — cannot carry stress if reduced
  • Heavy: branching rhyme — either
      (a) V (≠ [ə]) + coda (simple or complex)
      (b) long V or diphthong (± coda)
  • [ə] is weightless — never carries stress

"Heavy syllables attract stress" (cf. Week 12 §6.7).

Syllable position terminology
  • ult — the last syllable
  • penult — the syllable before the ult
  • antepenult — the syllable before the penult

probability [pɹ̣a.bə.bí.lə.ti]

pɹ̣a.bəbíləti
(pre-antepenult+)antepenultpenultult
교재 §7.1 (p. 180) 강세 규칙 도입 전 두 가지 전제: (1) Stressable syllable — light (non-branching rhyme, short V + no coda) vs heavy (branching: V+coda or long V/diphthong); [ə]는 weightless하여 강세 받을 수 없음. (2) Syllable position 용어 — ult (마지막), penult (끝에서 둘째), antepenult (끝에서 셋째). 교재 예시: probability [pɹ̣a.bə.bí.lə.ti] — anteрenult=bí, penult=lə, ult=ti. 이 두 정의가 §7.2 Penult Rule + §7.3 Ult Rule의 토대.

§7.2 Noun and Adjective Stress — The Penult Rule (1/2): Main Patterns

Penult Rule: In nouns/adjectives, stress falls on the penult if it is stressable (heavy = long V/diphthong, or coda; [ə] is weightless); otherwise, stress falls on the antepenult. (Yavaš pp. 180–183)

Group Pattern Examples
Disyll. NPenult stress (default, 81.7% per Hammond 1999)ágent, bálance, fáther, húsband, spínach, zípper
Disyll. AdjPenult stress (default, 81%)árid, cómmon, próper, hónest, sólid, ámple
3+ syll. (penult heavy)Penult stressed — long V/diphthong or codatomáto, aróma, horízon, agénda, compúter, barracúda
3+ syll. (penult light)Antepenult stressed (penult [ə] weightless)ábdomen, álgebra, ánimal, cómedy, vítamin, aspáragus, thermómeter
Hammond (1999) regularity: Trisyllabic nouns > 90% regular (42% penult, 49.5% antepenult of 2074 words). Trisyllabic adjectives 90%+ (75.3% penult, 15% antepenult of 666). The remaining ~10% are exceptions (next slide).
교재 §7.2 (pp.180–183) Penult Rule. Hammond (1999): 81.7% disyllabic 명사 + 81% disyllabic 형용사 = penult 강세. Trisyllabic+에서도 90%+ regularity (42% penult, 49.5% antepenult). Disyllabic penult 강세 예시: agent, balance, ballad, bottle, cabbage, chicken, cookie, country, father, husband. Disyllabic antepenult 강세 (light penult [ə]): tomato → toMAto, aroma, diploma, horizon, computer, bonanza. Trisyllabic+ antepenult 강세: abdomen, algebra, animal, buffalo, comedy, vitamin. 4+ syll: barracuda (penult heavy), asparagus/apocalypse/thermometer (antepenult). 예외 1 — Place-names: Yokohama, Vienna, Calcutta, Louisiana, Indiana, Montana 등 penult heavy 아닌데도 penult 강세. 추가 [ɑ]/[æ] penult: Morocco, Osaka, Chicago, Havana. 예외 2 — Real exceptions: typhoon, sardine, shampoo, antique, July (ult 강세). Also (mostly French borrowings): clarinet, cavalier, engineer, kangaroo, gasoline, cigarette, magazine, mayonnaise. 한국 학생들에게: '경향'으로 학습 권장 (90%+ regularity but exceptions exist).

§7.3 Verb Stress — The Ult Rule

Ult Rule: In verbs, stress falls on the final syllable (ult) if it is heavy (long vowel, diphthong, or ends in 2+ consonants); otherwise, stress falls on the penultimate syllable.

Ult stressed (heavy ult):

collapse → kəlǽps (CVC+C)

maintain → meɪntéɪn (diphthong)

exist → ɪɡzɪ́st

Penult stressed (light ult):

enter → ɛ́n.tɚ (ult = schwa)

bother → bɒ́ð.ɚ

travel → trǽv.əl

동사의 경우 '마지막 음절(ult)'이 무거우면 거기에 강세가 옵니다. 가벼우면 끝에서 두 번째 음절(penult)에 강세가 옵니다. 이 규칙을 통해 많은 영어 동사의 강세 위치를 예측할 수 있습니다. 단, 2음절 동사에서 이 규칙이 가장 명확하며, 다음 슬라이드의 명사/동사 쌍에서 실용적 대비를 확인합니다.

§7.4 Secondary Stress

Following Ladefoged (2001), the difference between primary and secondary stress is one of pitch (not stress per se). Both have prominence; only primary carries the major pitch change (= tonic accent).

Ladefoged 3-feature framework
Type±stress±tonic±full V
Primary+++
Secondary+−+
Unstressed−−± (often [ə])

Numerical system: 3 plus = 1 (primary), 2 plus = 2 (secondary), 1 plus = 3, 0 plus = 4.

photography = 4 1 4 3 · photographic = 2 4 1 4

Disyllabic words with both stresses

Primary–Secondary (post-tonic secondary):

cráyon, cýclone, fránchise, émpire, áspect, áccent, éncore, ásset, hýgiene

Secondary–Primary (pre-tonic secondary):

bàmbóo, sàrdíne, hòtél, tỳphóon, tàbóo, tràpéze, sùpréme, ràccóon, gòurmét

Rhythm rule: English avoids stress clash → secondary stress alternates rhythmically. Examples: education, organization, refrigerator, Afghanistan, reconciliation, excommunicate.
교재 §7.4 (pp.185–187) Secondary Stress. Ladefoged (2001) 핵심 통찰: primary vs secondary 차이는 stress가 아니라 **pitch** — 둘 다 prominence는 있지만 primary만 major pitch change (tonic accent) 동반. 3-feature framework: ±stress, ±tonic accent, ±full V → primary (+,+,+), secondary (+,−,+), unstressed (−,−,±). 숫자 시스템 1–4: 3plus=1 (primary), 2plus=2 (secondary), 1plus=3, 0plus=4. 예: photography [fə.tɑ́.gɹə.fi] = 4 1 4 3; photographic [fò.tə.gɹǽ.fɪk] = 2 4 1 4. Disyllabic 양쪽 강세 패턴: (a) Primary-Secondary (post-tonic): crayon, cyclone, franchise, empire, aspect, accent, encore, asset, hygiene — 첫 음절 primary, 둘째 secondary. (b) Secondary-Primary (pre-tonic): bamboo, sardine, hotel, typhoon, taboo, trapeze, supreme, raccoon, gourmet — 첫 음절 secondary, 둘째 primary. Rhythm rule: stress clash 회피 — secondary는 주기적 교대로 배치됨. 예: gratification [ɡɹæ̀.tə.fə.ké.ʃən], bibliography. 4+ syllable: Afghanistan [æ̀f.gæ.nə.stæ̀n], reconciliation, excommunicate.

§7.5 Affixes — Three Suffix Types

Prefixes do not change word stress. Suffixes fall into three classes by their effect on stress (Yavaš §7.5, p. 187):

§7.5.1
Stress-Bearing (Attracting)

Effect

Suffix attracts the stress (always heavy syllable).

Suffixes

-ade, -aire, -ation, -ee, -eer, -ese, -esque, -ette, -itis, -ific

Example

lemon → lemonáde

§7.5.2
Stress-Neutral

Effect

Base keeps original stress; no change.

Suffixes

All 8 inflectional (-s, -ed, -ing, -er, -est, ...) + derivational (-al, -ful, -ly, -ment, -ness, -ship, -y adj, ...)

Example

háppy → háppiness

§7.5.3
Stress-Shifting (Fixing)

Effect

Stress moves to syllable immediately before the suffix.

Suffixes

-ic, -ity, -ify, -ial, -ian, -ical, -icide, -ious, -ous, -ual, -ometer

Example

périod → periódic

Common element of (a) and (b) [(stress-bearing & stress-shifting)]: both change the location of stress; common element of (b) and (c) [(stress-shifting & stress-neutral)]: both do not carry stress.

접미사의 강세 영향은 영어 어휘 형태론에서 중요한 주제. 교재 §7.5의 세 가지 분류: (a) §7.5.1 Stress-bearing (attracting) — 접미사 자신에 강세, 항상 heavy syllable (-ade, -aire, -ation, -ee, -eer, -ese, -esque, -ette, -itis, -ific). e.g., lémon → lemonáde, Japán → Japanése. (b) §7.5.2 Stress-neutral — 어근 강세 유지. 모든 8개 inflectional (-s, -ed, -ing 등) + 다수 derivational (-al, -er, -ful, -hood, -ish, -ism, -less, -ly, -ment, -ness, -ship, adj-forming -y). (c) §7.5.3 Stress-shifting (fixing) — 접미사 직전 음절로 강세 이동 (-ic, -ity, -ify, -ial, -ian, -ical, -icide, -ious, -ous, -ual, -ometer 등). e.g., périod → periódic. 공통점: (a)+(b) 모두 강세 위치 변경; (b)+(c) 모두 강세를 carry 안 함. (a)+(c)는 root 강세 변경 발생. 학생들에게 -y 주의: 명사 형성 -y는 stress-shifting (homophone → homophony), 형용사 형성 -y는 stress-neutral (silk → silky).

(4) Pile-up: moment → momentary but momentarily — 다수 unstressed 음절 쌓이면 강세 antepenult로 이동.

Summary

Key Concepts — Week 13

  • Variable & Mobile Stress: English stress varies across syllables and shifts in morphologically related words (with vowel reduction)
  • Penult Rule (nouns/adj.): stress penult if heavy; otherwise antepenult (Hammond 81.7% disyll. nouns)
  • Ult Rule (verbs): stress ult if heavy; otherwise penult (Hammond 99% of disyll. verbs)
  • N–V Pairs: pérmit (N) vs. permít (V); stress shift signals grammatical category + may trigger vowel reduction
  • Secondary Stress: Ladefoged 3-feature framework (±stress, ±tonic accent, ±full V); rhythmic alternation avoids clash
  • Suffix Types: stress-bearing (-ee, -ese, -ation), stress-neutral (-ness, -ly, -ment), stress-shifting (-ic, -ity, -ify)
